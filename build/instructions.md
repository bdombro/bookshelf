## 3. Sand and finish

Sand both faces and visible edges of every pine panel, then apply the clear finish while panels are flat. Tape off glue areas: panel ends, the rear edges the back is glued to, and the shelf lines on the sides. Test on an offcut first.

## 5. Drill for confirmats

Uses: 184 confirmat screws, stepped drill bit.

Drill through each side into the middle of each panel's end, on its line, at most 12cm apart. Positions from the side's front edge:

- 25cm-deep panels: 8.7, 17, 25.3
- 29.5cm-deep panels: 4.9, 14.8, 24.6
- kickboard: 2 screws at heights 2.5 and 7.5, 6.2cm from the front edge

## 6. Assemble each carcass

Uses: wood glue, confirmat screws.

Work on the floor with the sides on their rear edges, front edges up:

1. Glue and screw the bottom and top between the two sides.
2. Add the cabinet top (wide) and the shelves, each on its marked line.
3. Fit the kickboard under the bottom, 5cm back from the front edge.
4. Leave the cabinet shelf until the back is on.

## 7. Square and fix the back

Uses: 266 staples in total, wood glue.

Measure both diagonals of the carcass and push it until they match:

- narrow: 215.4
- wide: 228.8

Pencil the shelf lines on the back, then glue and staple it to the rear edges of the sides, top, bottom and every shelf, every 12.5cm. Keep each staple's crown along the edge, not across it, so a leg can't split out through a face. The back runs to the floor behind the kickboard.

## 8. Cabinet shelf, hinges and doors

Uses: 8 hinges, 4 knobs, confirmat screws.

1. Fix the cabinet shelf against the back with its underside at 47.3, leaving 4.5cm at the front for the hinges.
2. Drill each door's hinge cups 10cm from its top and bottom ends, using the hinge maker's template for the edge distance.
3. Mount the plates on the sides to match: door bottom at 10.2, top at 86.8.
4. Hang the doors and adjust for an even 0.4cm gap between the pair.
5. Fit each knob 4cm from the door's inner edge and 6cm down from its top.

## 9. Install

Uses: 8 connector bolts, 4 anti-tip brackets, 8 plasterboard toggles.

1. Remove the skirting boards.
2. Stand each unit up with two people; a wide unit is a two-person lift.
3. Bolt each set's two units together through their touching sides, 14.8cm from the front edge, at heights 29.8, 101.5, 133.1, 196.1.
4. Fix each unit's anti-tip bracket through its top panel into a stud wherever possible (find them with a stud finder). Where there's no stud use spring or snap toggles, never plain plugs. A narrow unit tips very easily: never skip this.
